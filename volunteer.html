Interested in volunteering?

Bowl Club wouldn't be possible without our amazing fleet of volunteers. Volunteer shifts are usually around 2 hours, but we'd love to have you even if you only have 30 minutes to spare! Reach out to Cooper Carlson at cjcarlson@mines.edu for more details.

Ways to help

Cooking & prep

We do the majority of our cooking over the weekend. This role entails, chopping, prepping, and cooking meals.

Setup & cleanup

Setup begins at 4pm the day of. This role includes arranging tables and chairs, decorating, washing dishes, and preparing food into serving trays. We serve food until 8pm (or until it runs out). Cleanup includes washing dishes, cleaning the floor and tables, and packing up equipment.

What to expect

About a week in advance of the dinner, we will send out a volunteer availability form for you to fill out. From there, you will be added to a volunteer GroupMe and assigned a role according to your availability. If you will be working with food, we will reach out to you to complete an ~30 minute food safety training. Contact Cooper Carlson with any questions: cjcarlson@mines.edu We look forward to having you as apart of the team!
