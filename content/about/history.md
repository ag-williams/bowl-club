Bowl Club was established at the Colorado School of Mines in 2025 by Cooper and Allie. During their study abroad at the University of Otago in Dunedin, New Zealand, the two were inspired by [The Bowling Club](https://thebowlingclub.co.nz/), which brings volunteers together to cook and serve pay-as-you-can community dinners enjoyed by university students and the broader Dunedin community alike. 

Upon their return to Mines, Cooper and Allie, the leaders of Mines Curling and Plant Futures, saw a path toward taking the vibe of The Bowling Club home. By combining the community focus of Mines Curling with the sustainability of Plant Futures, they could cultivate the unique Mines community in a new way: Have student volunteers rescue, grow, and otherwise procure ingredients, then cook on campus and serve a homemade meal to anyone in the Mines community, all contained in one club.

With the help of funding from Philanthropy Tank at Mines, the quickly growing team was able to hold the first Bowl Club dinner on March 12, 2026. To date, we've held four Bowl Club dinners with the help of our amazing officer team, our volunteers, and everyone else who has made this possible.
